# Service Bulletin 0226 — Distrito Federal (DF)

Issued 2017-01-13.

Deliveries to Distrito Federal were affected by flooding on the primary access route. Committed deadlines for
parcels in transit to DF were suspended for 8 business days from
the issue date under section 3 of POL-SLA-001.

Compensation does not accrue for the suspension period. Parcels posted after
the suspension ended are subject to the standard Central-West baseline in
section 3 of POL-SHIP-001. Returns registered under code RC-301 during the
suspension window are re-classified to code RC-202 unless the purchaser
provides evidence of a separate delay.

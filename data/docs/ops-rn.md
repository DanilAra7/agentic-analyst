# Regional Operations Handbook — Rio Grande do Norte (RN)

## 1. Sorting hub

The designated sorting hub for Rio Grande do Norte is Natal. All parcels destined for
RN transit this hub regardless of origin. Parcels arriving after the
daily cut-off of 16:00 local time are processed on the following business
day and the committed deadline is counted from that day.

## 2. Business day definition for RN

Saturday is counted as a business day for parcels handled through the
Natal hub. In addition to national holidays, 5 state holidays are
observed in RN and are excluded from deadline counting. The full
calendar is published annually in the seller portal.

## 3. Free freight threshold

Orders shipped to RN qualify for free PAC-STD freight where the order
value net of discounts is BRL 300 or above. The threshold does not
apply to SEDEX-STD or SEDEX-12, and does not apply to the remote-area
municipalities listed in section 5.

## 4. Delivery attempts

The carrier makes 3 delivery attempts. After the final attempt the
parcel is held at the destination unit for 7 calendar days before
being returned to the seller at the seller's expense. Return-to-seller freight
is not reimbursed and is not covered by any code in section 4 of POL-RET-002.

## 5. Remote-area surcharge

4 municipalities in RN are classified as remote areas. A surcharge
of BRL 9.64 per parcel applies in addition to the rates published in
POL-RATE-001. Committed deadlines for remote areas are extended by 2 business
days beyond the Northeast baseline given in section 3 of POL-SHIP-001.

## 6. Escalation

Operational incidents affecting RN are escalated to the Northeast regional
coordinator. Delay compensation continues to accrue under POL-SLA-001 during
escalation unless a suspension event under section 3 of that document has been
recorded in the tracking history.

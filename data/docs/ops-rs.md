# Regional Operations Handbook — Rio Grande do Sul (RS)

## 1. Sorting hub

The designated sorting hub for Rio Grande do Sul is Porto Alegre. All parcels destined for
RS transit this hub regardless of origin. Parcels arriving after the
daily cut-off of 16:00 local time are processed on the following business
day and the committed deadline is counted from that day.

## 2. Business day definition for RS

Saturday is counted as a business day for parcels handled through the
Porto Alegre hub. In addition to national holidays, 3 state holidays are
observed in RS and are excluded from deadline counting. The full
calendar is published annually in the seller portal.

## 3. Free freight threshold

Orders shipped to RS qualify for free PAC-STD freight where the order
value net of discounts is BRL 250 or above. The threshold does not
apply to SEDEX-STD or SEDEX-12, and does not apply to the remote-area
municipalities listed in section 5.

## 4. Delivery attempts

The carrier makes 2 delivery attempts. After the final attempt the
parcel is held at the destination unit for 5 calendar days before
being returned to the seller at the seller's expense. Return-to-seller freight
is not reimbursed and is not covered by any code in section 4 of POL-RET-002.

## 5. Remote-area surcharge

25 municipalities in RS are classified as remote areas. A surcharge
of BRL 3.81 per parcel applies in addition to the rates published in
POL-RATE-001. Committed deadlines for remote areas are extended by 2 business
days beyond the South baseline given in section 3 of POL-SHIP-001.

## 6. Escalation

Operational incidents affecting RS are escalated to the South regional
coordinator. Delay compensation continues to accrue under POL-SLA-001 during
escalation unless a suspension event under section 3 of that document has been
recorded in the tracking history.

# How long until the money is back?

Card payments reverse over one or two billing cycles. Bank slips settle by transfer within ten business days. Vouchers are reissued in two.

## Applies to

This entry is written for buyers shipping to or from Paraná (PR).
Regional variations are set out in OPS-PR-001. Where this entry and a
policy document disagree, the policy document is authoritative: returns are
governed by POL-RET-002, shipping by POL-SHIP-001, delays by POL-SLA-001 and
settlement by POL-FIN-001.

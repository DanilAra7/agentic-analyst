# Service Bulletin 0051 — Rio Grande do Norte (RN)

Issued 2018-11-08.

Deliveries to Rio Grande do Norte were affected by temporary closure of the destination unit. Committed deadlines for
parcels in transit to RN were suspended for 7 business days from
the issue date under section 3 of POL-SLA-001.

Compensation does not accrue for the suspension period. Parcels posted after
the suspension ended are subject to the standard Northeast baseline in
section 3 of POL-SHIP-001. Returns registered under code RC-301 during the
suspension window are re-classified to code RC-202 unless the purchaser
provides evidence of a separate delay.

# My parcel is late. What am I owed?

Compensation starts once actual delivery passes the committed deadline. One to three days late means freight back in full; longer delays add a percentage of the item price.

## Applies to

This entry is written for buyers shipping to or from Tocantins (TO).
Regional variations are set out in OPS-TO-001. Where this entry and a
policy document disagree, the policy document is authoritative: returns are
governed by POL-RET-002, shipping by POL-SHIP-001, delays by POL-SLA-001 and
settlement by POL-FIN-001.

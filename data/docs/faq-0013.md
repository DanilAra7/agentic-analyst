# When does the delivery clock start?

On the first business day after the carrier physically receives the parcel, not on the day the order was placed. Seller handling time is counted on top.

## Applies to

This entry is written for buyers shipping to or from Rio Grande do Sul (RS).
Regional variations are set out in OPS-RS-001. Where this entry and a
policy document disagree, the policy document is authoritative: returns are
governed by POL-RET-002, shipping by POL-SHIP-001, delays by POL-SLA-001 and
settlement by POL-FIN-001.

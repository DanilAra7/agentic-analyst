# Service Bulletin 0181 — Rio Grande do Sul (RS)

Issued 2017-12-20.

Deliveries to Rio Grande do Sul were affected by temporary closure of the destination unit. Committed deadlines for
parcels in transit to RS were suspended for 5 business days from
the issue date under section 3 of POL-SLA-001.

Compensation does not accrue for the suspension period. Parcels posted after
the suspension ended are subject to the standard South baseline in
section 3 of POL-SHIP-001. Returns registered under code RC-301 during the
suspension window are re-classified to code RC-202 unless the purchaser
provides evidence of a separate delay.
